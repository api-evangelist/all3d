# ALL3D

> ALL3D is an AI-powered platform for generating product images, lifestyle visuals, videos, and 3D assets for ecommerce brands and agencies. Upload a product photo, describe your scene, and publish studio-quality visuals in minutes — no photography needed.

## What ALL3D Does

ALL3D replaces traditional product photography with an AI workflow. Brands upload a product photo and dimensions, build a scene using inspiration images and props, and generate 4K AI images accurate to real-world space. The platform also supports influencer-style video generation, product configurators, AR, and bulk asset creation.

## Core Capabilities

- **AI Product Images**: Studio shots and lifestyle scenes from a single product photo
- **AI Video**: Influencer-style and UGC-style video generation for social and ads
- **3D Precision Editing**: Convert products into editable 3D models for accurate camera angles and scene placement
- **Product Configurator**: Let customers explore colors, materials, and variations in real time
- **3D CGI & 360 Spins**: Full asset sets including 360-degree spins and all product variants
- **AR View in Room**: Customers can place products in their real space using augmented reality
- **Refine & Scale**: Magic erase, item replacement, scale adjustments, and bulk generation
- **Shopify & Amazon Integration**: Publish assets directly to your storefront

## Workflow (6 Steps)

1. **Add Product** — Upload a product photo and enter dimensions
2. **Build a Scene** — Add inspiration images, props, and style guidance
3. **Generate 4K Visuals** — AI generates photorealistic images accurate to real-world scale
4. **Refine & Scale** — Edit with built-in tools; scale to hundreds of SKUs
5. **Review & Collaborate** — Team-based review with approval/rejection workflow
6. **Publish to Store** — Direct Shopify and Amazon integration, or download

## Who It's For

- Ecommerce brands needing product imagery at scale
- Marketing and creative agencies producing lifestyle content
- Furniture, fashion, and consumer goods companies with large catalogs
- Enterprise teams requiring collaboration, API access, and managed services
